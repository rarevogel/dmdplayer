Library

Getting animations out of a table: tap Import and choose a .vpx or .vpt (Visual Pinball) or .fpt (Future Pinball) file. The app looks for the DMD images in the table and suggests animations. Loose GIFs, images, ZIP folders (such as FlexDMD/UltraDMD folders) and PinMAME/DMDExt dumps (.txt) work too.
